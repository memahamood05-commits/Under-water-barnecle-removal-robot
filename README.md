# Underwater Barnacle Removal Robot

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and development of an **Underwater Barnacle Removal Robot** intended to remove barnacles and other marine growth from submerged surfaces, particularly ship hulls.

Barnacle accumulation on underwater structures and ship hulls increases hydrodynamic drag, reduces fuel efficiency, and increases maintenance requirements. This robotic system aims to provide an efficient and practical approach to underwater surface cleaning while reducing the need for manual diver intervention.

## Project Objectives

* Design a robotic system capable of operating underwater.
* Develop a mechanical structure suitable for underwater cleaning operations.
* Explore an effective mechanism for removing barnacles and marine deposits.
* Improve the efficiency of underwater hull maintenance.
* Reduce manual effort and risks associated with underwater cleaning.

## Design & Development

The project includes the mechanical design and conceptual development of the robot, with a focus on its structure, component arrangement, and underwater cleaning mechanism.

The uploaded CAD design represents the proposed robotic configuration and serves as the foundation for further development and implementation.
